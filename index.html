How PARA! Works

From booking to drop-off in just a few taps. No calls, no waiting on the street.

1

Open the App

Launch PARA! and your current location is detected automatically. No setup needed.

2

Enter Destination

Type your drop-off or pick from saved locations like Home, School, or Market.

3

Book Your Ride

Confirm your fare estimate and tap Book Now. A nearby driver sees your request instantly.

4

Ride & Chat

Track your driver and chat directly in the app. No need to exchange numbers.
